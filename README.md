# PaySecure Cyber Readiness Posture Plan

An individual coursework project for BBM119L (Cybersecurity Risk Management and Governance), developing a full cybersecurity posture and Information Security Management System (ISMS) strategy for a fictional UK fintech start-up, **PaySecure**, a digital wallet and payment gateway provider.

## Scenario

PaySecure is scaling its operations and needs a comprehensive strategy to secure its information assets and meet compliance requirements. This project takes on the role of a cybersecurity consultant tasked with building that strategy end to end.

## Contents Covered

- **Business Context Analysis** — SWOT, stakeholder analysis, market and competitor review, ISMS scope
- **Governance Framework** — role definitions, organogram, hybrid risk ownership model, risk appetite statement
- **Risk Assessment** — comparative evaluation of risk methodologies (ISO 27005, NIST SP 800-30, OCTAVE), a risk matrix, prioritisation criteria, and a Return on Security Investment (ROSI) calculation justifying control spend
- **Security Controls** — Access Control and Asset Management policies, mapped to specific ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls
- **Incident Response Plan** — full incident lifecycle and incident management policy
- **Training and Awareness Program**
- **Compliance and Regulatory Considerations** — regulatory, operational, technology, and reporting compliance pillars
- **Continuous Improvement Strategy** — roadmap to ISO/IEC 27001 certification
